The Gateway to Norman

Where Norman comes to gather

Running west from the University of Oklahoma, Lindsey Street is one of Norman's busiest and most beloved corridors — a walkable stretch of local restaurants, salons, shops, fitness studios, and neighborhood staples. This is your guide to everything on the street.
