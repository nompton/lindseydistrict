Why the Lindsey corridor

Lindsey Street runs west from the University of Oklahoma through some of Norman's densest daytime traffic — a mile and a half of restaurants, retail, services, and anchor centers. High visibility, steady demand, and a built-in base of students, faculty, and neighbors make it one of the metro's most resilient commercial corridors.
